# cs4412-project
Project Title: "United States Federal Spending in Georgia"

Name: Seth Brice

Description: This project aims to explore the total amount of transactions made by the federal government to the state of Georgia in 2025. 
The goal is to explore what kind of businesses the federal government funds the most and what specific businesses recieve the majority of US tax dollars.
It answers three primary discovery questions through data mining using the apriori method, k-means clustering, and isolation forest. Within the documents, you can
expect to find the implemented methods, the results found from them, and my interpretations of these results.

Dataset Source: https://www.usaspending.gov/search?hash=c2c3c2289128b5b40f0f64f62e847828

Final Notebook: Accessed in the "Notebook" folder under "US Federal Spending GA Final Notebook"

Final Report: Accessed in the docs folder under "US Federal Spending in GA Final PDF"
